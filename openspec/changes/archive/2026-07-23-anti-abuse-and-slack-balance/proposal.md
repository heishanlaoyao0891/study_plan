# Anti-Abuse And Slack Balance

## Why

Leaderboards, levels, slack rewards, and makeup records create incentives to inflate study time. The product needs lightweight fairness controls without making personal/friends usage feel punitive.

## What Changes

- Add reasonable study-time and makeup limits.
- Make makeup consume slack minutes at a configurable ratio.
- Mark suspicious records for admin review instead of blocking normal usage aggressively.
- Clarify slack minutes as rest/activity balance, not money or redeemable value.
- Add weekly rest-balance suggestions based on study/slack history.

## Non-Goals

- No real-money penalties or rewards.
- No invasive anti-cheat tracking.
- No complex fraud-scoring system in MVP.
