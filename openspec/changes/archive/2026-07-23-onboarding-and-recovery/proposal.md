# Onboarding And Plan Recovery

## Why

The mini program now has multiple concepts: phone binding, plans, tasks, AI generation, timing, check-ins, slack minutes, reminders, and groups. New users need a short path to their first useful action, and returning users need recovery help when they fall behind.

## What Changes

- Add a lightweight first-run onboarding flow.
- Guide users through phone binding, optional reminder subscription, first plan creation or AI generation, and today's first task.
- Add a recovery flow for users who missed several days or accumulated overdue tasks.
- Allow users to reschedule future unfinished tasks through AI or rule-based planning.

## Non-Goals

- No long marketing landing page.
- No forced tutorial for every feature.
- No automatic deletion of overdue tasks without user confirmation.
